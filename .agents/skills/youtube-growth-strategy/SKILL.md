# YouTube Growth Strategy Skill

Develops data-backed growth roadmaps tailored to Top-Tier English-speaking markets (United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia).

## Strategic Framework

1. **Top-Tier Audience Windows**:
   - Calculate synchronized peak viewing hours across time zones:
     - US Eastern: 12:00–14:00 (Lunch) and 19:00–21:00 (Evening Prime)
     - UK / Western Europe: 17:00–19:00 GMT/BST
     - Bangladesh Time (Creator TZ): 22:00–00:00 (Prime slot)
   - Account for Daylight Saving Time (DST) offsets between US (EDT/EST) and UK (BST/GMT).

2. **Private Upload Buffer**:
   - Require video upload as PRIVATE 1–2 hours before public release.
   - Allows YouTube backend systems to process 1080p/4K transcode streams, content ID, and synthetic media tags.

3. **30-Day Content Experiment**:
   - Phase 1 (Days 1–10): Establish format baseline (3 Shorts/week).
   - Phase 2 (Days 11–20): Test hook variations (curiosity vs surprise vs macro cutaway).
   - Phase 3 (Days 21–30): Double down on outlier topics and adjust release windows based on early Studio 48h view velocity.
