# Competitor Intelligence Skill

Analyzes competitor ecosystems without copying or plagiarizing content.

## Investigation Methodology

1. **Breakout Creator Discovery**:
   - Identify 10–20 relevant creators in the target niche.
   - Prioritize smaller and emerging creators with unusually successful recent videos rather than vanity subscriber totals.

2. **Outlier Video Analysis**:
   - Calculate view-to-subscriber ratios (>3x channel average indicates a format breakthrough).
   - Document publication date, title structure, and core promise.

3. **Retention Hook Deconstruction**:
   - Dissect the first 3 seconds (visual contrast, curiosity cliffhanger, tension setup).
   - Note pacing, sound cues, and scene transitions.

4. **Originality Differentiation**:
   - Identify what competitors missed: unanswered viewer comments, poor visual fidelity, or lack of tactile 3D clarity.
   - Formulate unique narrative angles for YouTube AI Factory production.
