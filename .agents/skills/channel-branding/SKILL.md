# Channel Branding Skill

Crafts cohesive, professional branding packages for new and rebranded YouTube channels.

## Deliverables per Channel

1. **Brand Identity**:
   - 5–10 catchy name candidates + recommended name
   - Available YouTube handle candidates (`@handle`)
   - Positioning statement & channel content promise
   - SEO-rich About Bio (description) with upload schedule and call to action

2. **Metadata & Studio Configuration**:
   - Channel tags / keywords formatted as a comma-separated list for YouTube Studio
   - 3–5 strategic playlist titles and descriptions

3. **Visual Prompts (Digen / Image Generator)**:
   - **Avatar Prompt**: Square 1:1, centered, iconic character or mechanical emblem, stylized 3D render.
   - **Banner Prompt**: Panoramic 2560×1440, safe-area centered (1546×423), deep cinematic backdrop, zero clutter.
   - **Brand Colors & Typography Guide**: Primary, accent, and shadow palette codes with recommended font styles.

4. **First 30 Content Ideas**:
   - 30 categorized episode ideas to eliminate creator block during launch.
