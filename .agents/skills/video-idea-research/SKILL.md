# Video Idea Research Skill

Transforms niche trends and competitor insights into actionable, original video episode concepts.

## Concept Criteria

1. **Viewer Relevance & Curiosity**:
   - Focus on unanswered everyday questions, hidden mechanical wonders, tactile miniature stories, or sci-fi discoveries.
   - Establish a clear 1-sentence payoff: what the viewer discovers by watching until the end.

2. **Digen Production Feasibility**:
   - Formulate ideas naturally executable in 3–4 coherent scenes.
   - Estimate Digen generation credit requirements (typically 1 scene = 1–2 generation attempts).

3. **Output Format**:
   - Proposed Title
   - Target Viewer & Format (9:16 Shorts or 16:9 Long-form)
   - 1-Sentence Promise
   - 3 Opening Hook Candidates
   - Scene-by-Scene Outline (0–8s, 8–16s, 16–24s, 24–32s)
   - Estimated Credit Cost and Production Complexity Score (1–100)
