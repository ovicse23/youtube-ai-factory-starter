# Scriptwriting Skill

Authors crisp, engaging narration scripts matched to Digen AI video scene durations.

## Timing & Cadence Standards

- **Shorts (30–32s)**: 65–75 words total (approximately 2.2 words per second).
- **Shorts (60s)**: 130–145 words total.
- **Long-Form (3–5 min)**: 450–700 words with defined act breaks.

## Scene Breakdown Structure

Each script must be mapped to discrete visual scenes:
- **Scene 1 (Hook / Setup, 0–8s)**: Gripping visual reveal matching the chosen hook.
- **Scene 2 (Escalation / Cutaway, 8–16s)**: Inner mechanism, conflict, or micro-action.
- **Scene 3 (Climax / Reveal, 16–24s)**: Critical turnaround or technical explanation.
- **Scene 4 (Payoff / Resolution, 24–32s)**: Satisfying conclusion, lesson, or call to subscribe.

All narration must be natural, informative, and free of filler words.
