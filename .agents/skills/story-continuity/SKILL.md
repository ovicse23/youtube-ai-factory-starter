# Story Continuity Skill

Preserves coherent visual identity and world-building across episodic YouTube series.

## Continuity Enforcement

1. **Character Reference Records**:
   - Store detailed descriptions: physical appearance, clothing, material textures, scale references, and distinct color accents.
   - Example (Wick): *"Acorn-sized woodland lantern-keeper crafted from russet oak leaves and weathered brass, moss-covered bell-cap hat, glowing hexagonal brass lantern."*

2. **Style Bible Directives**:
   - Embed universal continuity prompts in every Digen scene prompt:
     `GLOBAL CONTINUITY: Original stylized 3D visual continuity for [Channel Name]. Coherent lighting, consistent proportions in every shot, zero text.`

3. **Lighting & Palette Memory**:
   - Keep shadows, highlights, and camera perspective consistent between adjacent scenes (e.g. twilight moss greens vs warm amber lantern glow).
