# Viral Hook Development Skill

Develops high-performing opening hooks specifically designed to capture attention in the first 3 seconds of a YouTube video or Short.

## The 3-Hook Blueprint

For every video idea, produce 3 distinct hook variations:

1. **Surprise / Paradox Hook**:
   - Challenges common intuition or presents an unexpected physical fact.
   - Example: *"There is an intentional hole drilled right through your airplane window—and it keeps the cabin pane from shattering."*

2. **High-Stakes Countdown Hook**:
   - Introduces urgency, extreme pressure, or life-saving engineering.
   - Example: *"At 35,000 feet, three thin layers hold back freezing air thanks to a one-millimeter bleed hole."*

3. **Macro Cutaway / Curiosity Hook**:
   - Prompts the viewer to look inside an everyday object or hidden corner.
   - Example: *"Slice an airliner window in half and you'll spot a tiny tunnel doing a life-saving job."*

## Quality Checklist
- Zero misleading clickbait.
- Hook audio matches first visual scene prompt.
- Pacing delivered in under 8 words / 3 seconds.
